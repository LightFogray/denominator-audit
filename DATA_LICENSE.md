# Data licensing

The **processed analysis data** in `data/processed/` are released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), to the
extent permitted by the terms of the original data providers.

The original crime, trip, population, boundary and deprivation data remain
subject to their providers' terms. They are **not** redistributed here except
for the small public NYC taxi-zone boundary file. Sources and attribution are
listed in `docs/data_provenance.md`.

If you reuse the processed data, cite the manuscript and the original data
providers listed in that file.
